Eighty Leads Produced Four Jobs. The Ads Are Not The Problem.

You said it yourself: ten pounds a lead is no good if they do not answer the phone.

Eighty leads in six weeks. Sixteen answered. Four went ahead. That is a 20% contact rate and a 5% conversion. Your first campaign was better and you could not say why.

We would not touch the ads. The filming is good, the spend is modest, and you turned over roughly £800,000 through those leads since February. What is broken sits between the click and the phone call.

No pricing until you have read this • Nothing paid up front • Your ad team stays
Before Anything Else — What Happened With TNW Will Not Happen Here
WHAT YOU TOLD US GBP12,000 paid up front, two years ago "A full in-house system. Once you've paid it, you'll never pay for a lead again." They moved to Dubai. You never heard from them. You still have the contract. You never got the money back. Their setters rang you again recently. HOW WE WORK No large payment up front. Nothing like twelve thousand. We are not selling you a system to run yourself. We are in the UK, and you can find us. Month to month. Stop it whenever you like.
Said first, because it should be

You have already paid twelve thousand pounds to someone who promised a system and vanished. Anyone selling you marketing has to answer that before they say anything else, and most will skip past it.

The part of their pitch that should have been the warning was “you will never pay for a lead again.” Nobody can promise that. We are not going to, and you should be suspicious of anyone who does — including us, if we ever start.

Finding 1 — The Numbers Say Friction, Not Traffic
YOUR LAST SIX WEEKS Leads generated 80 Answered the phone 16 (20%) Went ahead 4 (5%) Cost per lead, as reported GBP10 Cost per JOB GBP200 in media -- but 76 chases WHAT THAT SAME SPEND LOOKED LIKE BEFORE Average cost per lead GBP35 Your words: "we were just snowballed with people that wanted to go ahead" The lead got four times cheaper and four times worse.
This is the whole diagnosis in one line

A cheaper lead is not a better lead, and your own history proves it. When you were paying £35 the campaign was snowballing. At £10 you are getting people who filled a form in on a night shift because they were bored — your example, not ours.

The pixel is learning from whoever converts. If the cheapest, least serious enquiry is what it sees as a conversion, it goes and finds more of exactly that person, week after week. That is why campaign two is worse than campaign one, and why new creative alone will not fix it.

And six of last week's enquiries were other installers checking your prices. Your form asks enough to make that worth their while, and nothing on it tells them not to bother.

Finding 2 — Nothing Is Measured, So Nobody Can Prove Anything
WHAT WE LOOKED FOR ON YOUR SITE RESULT Google Analytics / GA4 not found Conversion tracking not found Call tracking not found Local business structured data not found Service markup not found Website listed on MCS register link not working Meta pixel installed, but not configured properly -- and it only holds 180 days of data
You are paying two suppliers and neither can show you a job

The ad company reports leads and cost per lead. The SEO company reports rankings. Neither can tell you which of your four jobs came from where, because nothing on the site records it.

That is also why you cannot judge the £700 a month on SEO. You told us the ad company ran their own software over it and suggested the money might be better spent elsewhere — which is exactly what a company that also sells SEO would say. Without tracking, you have no way to referee that.

Install measurement and the argument settles itself. It is the cheapest thing on this list and everything else depends on it.

Finding 3 — What We Would Actually Build
1. A LANDING PAGE BETWEEN THE AD AND THE FORM Not a form on its own. A page that says who this is for, what a system costs, what happens on a survey, and what you will not do. Video, not just text. People who are not serious leave here, for free. 2. ANSWERS TO THE QUESTIONS THEY ARE ALREADY ASKING What does it actually cost. How long does it take. What happens on the day. Do I need scaffolding. Will it work on my roof. Short videos, your face. Educated buyers answer the phone. Cold ones do not. 3. A FORM THAT SORTS THEM BEFORE YOU RING Roof type, rough usage, timescale, and whether they own the property. Scored one to four. You ring the fours first. You stop ringing the ones. 4. MEASUREMENT, SO THIS STOPS BEING AN OPINION GA4, conversion tracking, call tracking, and a number against each channel. 5. THE SITE FIXES MCS register link, local business and service markup, and a decision on commercial versus domestic.
Your ad company keeps doing what it is good at

We are not asking you to sack anybody. They film well, they spend sensibly, and the creative is not the problem. You told us new content from Corfu is going live imminently — that should run.

What we would change is where that traffic lands. At the moment a video ad sends somebody straight to a form. Nothing in between explains anything, so the people who arrive know nothing and the ones who are curious rather than serious sail straight through.

Finding 4 — Decide What You Are Selling
Companies House lists you as a commercial builder. Your website sells domestic solar. You told us you are looking into commercial, and that you are buying commercial leads from a portal at GBP160-GBP300 each. NOBODY IS BIDDING ON GOOGLE FOR THIS IN YOUR AREA. Meta is the wrong channel for commercial. Search is the right one, and the ground is empty.
This is the part worth more than the lead-quality fix

You are already paying £160 to £300 a lead for commercial work on somebody else's portal. Those buyers are searching, not scrolling, and no competitor near you is bidding for them.

But the site cannot take them today, because it reads as a domestic installer with a commercial registration behind it. One clear commercial page, separated from the domestic journey, is what makes that channel usable.

Your 26 town and service pages are the best structural thing you have — Seed Renewables, SunSmart and BlackTech have none between them. That work is already done and it is worth building on.

What We Are Not Going To Claim
We will not promise you a booking rate. We aim for 50%. You are at 20%. We think it should be better than 20% and we will not put a number on it. We will not promise a cost per lead. Ours typically run GBP40-GBP80, not GBP10. A dearer lead that answers is cheaper than a cheap one that does not. We will not tell you to stop paying your ad company. We will not ask for twelve thousand pounds up front.
You rated what we showed you a ten. That is the part we have to be careful with.

A ten out of ten on a first call is usually where somebody starts overpromising. You have been there once already and it cost you twelve thousand pounds.

So the honest position: the diagnosis is solid and the fixes are ordinary. Measurement, a page that educates, a form that sorts, and a decision about commercial. None of it is clever. All of it is missing.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Measurement first. GA4, conversion tracking, call tracking, a number per channel. Until this exists nobody can referee the ads against the SEO, including us.
  • The landing page and the answer videos. This is the piece that changes who picks up the phone.
  • The form, scored one to four, so you stop spending evenings ringing people who were bored.
  • The site fixes — MCS link, structured data, and the commercial-versus-domestic decision.
  • Then, and only then, more spend. Doubling the budget today doubles the tyre-kickers. You said the same thing yourself about the last time you scaled it.

Sixteen out of eighty answered the phone. That is the number to move.

Not the cost per lead, and not the volume. The ads already work — what happens next does not.
